# cite-viewer

Builds a page on your own computer that shows your draft with every citation as a link. Click a citation and the cited page from the source opens beside it, with the relied-on words shaded and a status:

- **Supported** (green): the passage says what your sentence says.
- **Check** (red): a misquote, a wrong pin, or a sentence that overstates the source, with the fix.
- **No pincite** (amber): a cite to specific material with no pin.
- **Source not loaded** (gray): anything Claude could not load.

Legal statements with no citation are marked too. Record cites (a reporter’s record, ROA pages, docket entries, a joint appendix) can be linked to the record files where they already sit on your computer.

## What you need

- The sources as PDFs. Westlaw users can let Claude pull them with Find & Print (charged under your plan). Lexis or other users put one PDF per authority in a folder.
- A computer (not a phone or tablet) where the PDFs and the viewer can be saved.
- Claude with access to a folder on your computer, and a browser if Claude is pulling from Westlaw.

## How it fits your process

Run it once the text is settled and the citations have been through a cite-check and a citator, before the final proofread. It does not replace reading the cases or running KeyCite or Shepard’s; it makes the reading fast and records what each passage supports.

## Confidentiality

The viewer holds your draft and, with record links, points to record material. It is built to stay on your computer as a file you open by double-clicking. Do not publish or host it.
