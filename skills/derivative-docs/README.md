# derivative-docs

Builds the documents that travel with a main pleading: an appendix, a stay motion or application, an IFP motion, a proposed order, or record excerpts. It reads the main pleading first and copies the caption, cause numbers, party styling, counsel block, and requested stay word for word, so the companion matches.

It ends with two tables: where each carried-over item came from, and each court-rule requirement marked present, missing, or needing your confirmation.

## Courts covered

The checklists cover Texas capital habeas practice: the U.S. Supreme Court, the Fifth Circuit, the Texas Court of Criminal Appeals, Texas district courts, and the Southern, Northern, Eastern, and Western Districts of Texas.

Practicing elsewhere? Keep everything above the checklists in SKILL.md and replace the checklists with your own courts’ rules.

## Verify the rules

Court rules change. Confirm every rule in the checklists against the court’s current rules before relying on it.
